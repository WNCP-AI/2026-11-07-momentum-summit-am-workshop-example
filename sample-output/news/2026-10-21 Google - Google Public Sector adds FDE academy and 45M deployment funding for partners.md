At its Partner Connect event, Google Public Sector launched a Forward Deployed Engineer academy for partners,
expanded deployment and adoption funding to $45M to pay for partner services across the US public sector, and
offered ISVs up to $1M toward FedRAMP and DoD accreditation costs. It came out the evening of our leadership call
with Sundar, Morgan and Taylor, and none of our calls mention it. First run: maybe, because it is about
Google, not GV. After the J6 change Google counts as the firm, and this is a programme for deployment partners and
forward deployed engineers in government. Yes.

> "Expanding our investment pool to $45M in dedicated deployment and adoption funding, designed to pay for partner
> professional services to deploy Gemini across the entirety of the U.S. public sector for the first time."
