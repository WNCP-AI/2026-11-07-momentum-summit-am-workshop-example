# Product

## WNCP AI
- Embedded AI Advisory: a fractional AI product executive on retainer, for VC funds, founders and product-led companies.
- Executive AI Immersion: hands-on sessions on the client's real problems. Output: working workflows and a readout.
- Innovation Sprint: 2 to 5 working AI prototypes, with specs and business cases.

## FDE4Real
- A pin or wrist watch captures a consultant's client meetings.
- It syncs to a private server the client runs. The server filters private data, then builds docs, memories and a
  team knowledge base.
- The team uses that knowledge base to map each client's needs and decide how to deploy AI tools.
- Runs on a custom-trained model the company says has a near-0% hallucination rate. Cleared for child-safety and
  top-secret environments.
- Today: live. Next version adds agentic development and internal enterprise software factories.
