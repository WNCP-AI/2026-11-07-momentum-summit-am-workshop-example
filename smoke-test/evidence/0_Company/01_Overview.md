# Overview

## One-liner
WNCP AI is "Your AI Expert in Residence": it embeds AI practitioners in client organizations to build with the team
and hand over the skills.

## Identity
- Three offerings: Embedded AI Advisory, Executive AI Immersion and Innovation Sprint.
- Method: scope the right problems, ship working software, then scale what works.
- FDE4Real is a WNCP AI subsidiary: the AI wearable for forward-deployed engineering teams.

- FDE4Real: live, with revenue. Raising a strategic round (see `05_Traction.md`).
