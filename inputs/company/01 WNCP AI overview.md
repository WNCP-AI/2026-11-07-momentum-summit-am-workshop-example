## What WNCP AI does

- The tagline is "Your AI Expert in Residence." ([wncp.ai](https://wncp.ai))
- It embeds AI practitioners in client organizations. They build alongside the team and hand over the skills.
  ([wncp.ai](https://wncp.ai))

## Three ways to work with WNCP AI

- **Embedded AI Advisory.** A fractional AI product executive for VC funds, founders and product-led companies. The
  work covers technical diligence, product strategy and build-versus-buy decisions, on a retainer.
  ([wncp.ai/offerings](https://wncp.ai/offerings))
- **Executive AI Immersion.** Hands-on sessions for executive teams, using the client's real problems. The output is
  working workflows and a written readout of the best AI opportunities. ([wncp.ai/offerings](https://wncp.ai/offerings))
- **Innovation Sprint.** A build sprint for product and engineering teams. It produces 2 to 5 working AI-enabled
  prototypes, with specs and business cases. ([wncp.ai/offerings](https://wncp.ai/offerings))

## Method

WNCP AI works in three steps: scope the right problems, ship working software, then scale what works.
([wncp.ai/methodology](https://wncp.ai/methodology))
