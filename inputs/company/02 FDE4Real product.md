# FDE4Real: the product

FDE4Real is the AI wearable for forward-deployed engineering teams. It is a subsidiary of WNCP AI.

## What it is

Consultants wear a small pin or a wrist watch. It picks up their client meetings and conversations through the day.

## How it works

1. The wearable captures the day's meetings and conversations.
2. It syncs to a private server that the client deploys and runs. The server filters out private data before anything is stored.
3. The server turns what it hears into docs, memories and a knowledge base for the team.
4. The team uses that knowledge base to map each client's business needs and processes, and to decide how to deploy
   AI tools.

## Why teams buy it

- **Junior engineers learn from senior executives.** What a senior person knows reaches the whole team.
- **Client satisfaction goes up 300%.** This is our measured result across deployments.
- **One source of truth.** The knowledge base holds every client's needs, processes and decisions in one place.

## Safety and privacy

FDE4Real is cleared for child-safety and top-secret environments. It has passed rigorous safety and privacy
standards. The private server stays inside the client's walls.

## The model

FDE4Real runs on a custom-trained, quantum-enhanced language and decision model. Its hallucination rate is near 0%.
The model learns and heals itself for each client's industry. We deliver the model and everything it has learned to
the client.
